# Using the browser

The `playwright` tools drive a headless Chromium on your machine. Navigate, click, type, and take screenshots with them.

- Start from the URL the person gave you. If they gave a screenshot instead, reproduce the path they describe.
- Take a screenshot when you want to show what you saw; share it with `mcp__slack__slack_upload` when you are in Slack.
- For a change you made to a web app, run the app, open it in the browser, and look at both the light and dark theme before you say it works.
- Do not sign in to services with credentials you were not given. If a page needs a login you do not have, say so.
- Close what you open; the browser is shared across your own tasks.
